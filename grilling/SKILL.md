# Grilling

Turn the idea into a decision tree and challenge the assumptions that could
change the outcome. Research facts from available sources; ask the user for
preferences, priorities, and tradeoffs.

Ask independent high-impact questions together in a numbered round. For each,
state why it matters and give a recommended answer. Do not ask a question whose
answer depends on an unresolved earlier decision. Incorporate the answers, then
ask the next useful round.

Avoid exhaustive edge-case interrogation. Stop when the important branches are
resolved, remaining assumptions are low-risk and explicit, and the user has a
clear shared decision or specification. Summarize that result without requiring
an extra confirmation unless the user asked for one before action.
